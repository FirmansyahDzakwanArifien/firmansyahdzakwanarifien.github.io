Navigation & sidebar

Minimize / maximize

On a laptop or desktop, the Minimize button at the bottom of the sidebar shrinks it to an icon rail and gives the page more room. Press it again (it now says Maximize) to bring the full sidebar back. Your choice is remembered on this device.

Peek while minimized

With the sidebar minimized, rest the cursor on it: it slides open over the page so you can read the labels and open the Blog, Tools, and Playground groups. Move the cursor away and it tucks back in. Keyboard users get the same when Tab reaches the sidebar.

On phones and tablets

The sidebar becomes a drawer. Tap in the top bar to open it, and tap outside it to close it. The top bar also holds search and the command palette.

Search & command palette

Search

Type in the search box in the top bar to search every post by title, tag, and content. Results appear as you type; Esc or Cancel closes them.

Command palette

Ctrl K (⌘ K on a Mac) jumps to any page, post, project, lab, or certification, and runs actions such as copying my email or printing the CV. Use ↑ ↓ and Enter.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcuts work anywhere except while you are typing in a field. Press ? to see them on any page.

Terminal

Press ` (the key left of 1) for a small terminal that answers questions about me. Tab completes commands, ↑ recalls the previous one, and exit or Esc closes it.

Themes

Light & dark

The site follows your system setting. Switch it with the button at the bottom of the sidebar or with d.

Hacker theme

A green-on-black terminal look. Turn it on or off with x, the command palette, or theme hacker in the terminal.
