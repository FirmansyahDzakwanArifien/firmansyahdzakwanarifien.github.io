## Why It Matters

The NSE program is Fortinet's structured path for validating both conceptual security knowledge and practical FortiGate/Security Fabric skills, and it's widely recognized across enterprise and service-provider environments that run Fortinet's Next-Generation Firewalls. Progressing through NSE 1–5 moves from "understanding the threat landscape" to "operating and monitoring a Fortinet-secured environment" — directly complementing hands-on experience gained in SOC analyst and infrastructure roles, and building toward the more advanced NSE tracks (NSE 6/7) focused on specialized product and architect-level expertise.
