## Introduction

That morning, a giant **"5th Edition"** banner greeted us in the lobby of **Hotel Bidakara, Jakarta**. For the next two days (**October 28-29, 2026**), my friends and I spent our time at **National Cybersecurity Connect 2026 (NCSC 2026)**.

I came home with one lesson: **cybersecurity, in the end, is about protecting trust.**

## Main Stage: Money Cannot Restore Trust

On the main stage, one line on the screen made me stop taking notes:

> "Uang tidak bisa mengembalikan kepercayaan." (Money cannot restore trust.)
{: .prompt-warning }

Building trust is cheap. Restoring it is almost impossible, especially when the system that failed is an AI system.

![Main stage panel slide about trust](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/firmansyahdzakwanarifien/firmansyahdzakwanarifien-assets@main/blog/img/album/posts/NCSC2026/main-stage-trust.jpg){: width="420" }
_Main stage panel session_

### When AI Fails, Recover With:

From the SAWAH Cyber Security session on the main stage:

1. **Be open and transparent**: Show what went wrong and why
2. **Be accountable**: A person takes responsibility, not "the system"
3. **Fix it and prove it**: Repair the AI and its data, then test it again
4. **Compensate fairly**: Help the people affected, quickly and accurately

## Cyber Stage: Attacks That Exploit Trust

The cyber stage had short, dense sessions. One covered attacks that hide behind a **familiar email**, where the attacker abuses the trust we already give to people and brands we know. Another walked through the threat landscape in the **AI era**.

![Cyber stage session on exploiting trust](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/firmansyahdzakwanarifien/firmansyahdzakwanarifien-assets@main/blog/img/album/posts/NCSC2026/cyber-stage-trust.jpg){: width="420" }
_Cyber stage: exploiting trust_

> Both stages told the same story from different sides: attackers target trust, and defenders have to protect and rebuild it.
{: .prompt-tip }

## Live Demo: Our Faces Are Data

At one booth, a face recognition camera identified visitors in real time. Registered visitors got their names on screen within seconds, and the rest were labelled **"Tidak dikenal"** (not recognised).

![Face recognition demo](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/firmansyahdzakwanarifien/firmansyahdzakwanarifien-assets@main/blog/img/album/posts/NCSC2026/face-recognition.jpg){: width="420" }
_Live face recognition demo on the expo floor_

It was fun to watch, but also a reminder that biometric data needs protecting too. Our faces are data.

## Expo Floor: One Floor, Many Solutions

The expo floor covered SOC services, managed security, and certifications. It was a good chance to talk directly with the teams behind the products, including **Alphacode**, **SAWAH**, **CompTIA**, **CrowdStrike x Virtus**, and **embed/it** with their Agentic SOC as a Service.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Trust is the asset**: Security controls exist to protect the trust users give to a system
2. **AI raises the stakes**: When an AI system fails, transparency and accountability matter as much as the fix
3. **Attackers exploit familiarity**: A familiar sender is not a verified sender
4. **Biometrics are sensitive data**: Convenience from face recognition comes with a privacy cost
