## About the Certification

**Fortinet NSE 1 — Certified in Cybersecurity** is the first level of the Fortinet Network Security Expert (NSE) program. It is an awareness-level certification aimed at anyone who works with information systems, establishing the shared vocabulary and mental model that the rest of the NSE track builds on.

## What It Covers

- **Threat landscape** — who the threat actors are (cybercriminals, hacktivists, nation-states, insiders) and what motivates them.
- **Common attack types** — malware, phishing and social engineering, ransomware, denial of service, and how attacks typically unfold.
- **Business impact** — the cost of breaches, the value of data, and why security is an organizational responsibility rather than only an IT one.
- **Security fundamentals** — basic hygiene such as strong authentication, patching, least privilege, and awareness training.

## Why It Matters

NSE 1 is deliberately non-technical, but it frames every later level: before configuring a firewall policy or reading SOC logs, it helps to understand *what* is being defended against and *why*. It also formalizes the awareness side of security that I apply when mentoring students and running community workshops.

This certification continues the Fortinet track started with the [Fortinet Certified Associate (FCA)]({% post_url 2026-05-01-fortinet-certified-associate %}) and is followed by [NSE 2]({% post_url 2026-08-04-fortinet-nse2-certified-cybersecurity %}).
