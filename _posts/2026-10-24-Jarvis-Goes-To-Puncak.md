## Introduction

Most days, I meet my colleagues at **PT Jarvis Integrasi Solusi** in the office, on a client site, or in a work group chat full of tickets and change windows. On **October 23-24, 2026**, we met somewhere different: at a lunch table, on the Go-Luge track, and in front of a stove at the villa.

This was **Jarvis Goes To Puncak**, our team outbound in Puncak, Bogor. Two simple days, but a lot of fun to spend together.

## Day One: Gathering and Lunch

We started on Friday at **Matea Coffee**, the meeting point before everyone headed to Friday prayers. It was the first time in a while that the whole team was in one place without a laptop open.

After prayers, we had lunch together at **Gumati Puncak**. Sharing one long table turned out to be a better icebreaker than any planned activity.

![Lunch at Gumati Puncak](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/firmansyahdzakwanarifien/firmansyahdzakwanarifien-assets@main/blog/img/album/posts/jarvis-puncak-2026/gumati-lunch.jpg){: width="700" }

Then we checked in at **Villa Almeera**, our home for the night.

## Go-Luge: Racing Until Dark

The highlight of the trip was **Go-Luge** in Cisarua. Tickets, gloves, a short briefing, and then we were lined up at the start under the green neon lights. We rode from the afternoon until it got dark.

![The team lined up at the Go-Luge start](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/firmansyahdzakwanarifien/firmansyahdzakwanarifien-assets@main/blog/img/album/posts/jarvis-puncak-2026/goluge-start-line.jpg){: width="700" }

## Night: Barbecue at the Villa

Back at the villa, we prepped dinner in the kitchen and kept the barbecue going until late at night. No agenda, no slides, just food and stories.

![Cooking at the villa](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/firmansyahdzakwanarifien/firmansyahdzakwanarifien-assets@main/blog/img/album/posts/jarvis-puncak-2026/villa-cooking.jpg){: width="420" }
_Prepping for the barbecue night_

## Key Takeaways

1. **Teams are built outside tickets too**: Working with people is easier once you have shared a table and a track with them
2. **Simple is enough**: Good food, one fun activity, and a night together made the trip memorable
3. **Rest is part of the job**: Stepping away from the server room for two days makes coming back easier

## Final Thoughts

Thank you to everyone at Jarvis for these two days in Puncak. I hope we can get together like this again soon.
