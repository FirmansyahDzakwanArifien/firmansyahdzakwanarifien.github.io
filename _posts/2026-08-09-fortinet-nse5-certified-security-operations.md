## About the Certification

**Fortinet NSE 5 — Certified in Security Operations** shifts the focus from configuring a single firewall to operating a whole Fortinet-secured environment: collecting logs centrally, correlating events, managing many devices consistently, and turning the data into reports and response actions. It is the level closest to real SOC work.

## What It Covers

- **FortiAnalyzer** — log collection from Security Fabric devices, log storage and retention, event handlers, and incident management.
- **Analytics and reporting** — FortiView, building and scheduling reports, and using log data to answer "what happened" questions quickly.
- **FortiManager** — centralized configuration and policy management for FortiGate fleets, ADOMs, and change control.
- **Security operations workflow** — from alert to triage to response, and how automation (playbooks, Fabric connectors) reduces manual toil.

## Why It Matters

My background includes SOC analyst work — monitoring traffic and logs, investigating incidents, and coordinating response. NSE 5 formalizes that experience on the Fortinet stack and closes the loop opened by NSE 1: from understanding the threat landscape to actually detecting and responding to it in production. It completes NSE 1–5 and sets up the specialist NSE 6/7 tracks.
