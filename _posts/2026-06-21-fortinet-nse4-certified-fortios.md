## About the Certification

**Fortinet NSE 4 — Certified in FortiOS** is the first hands-on technical level of the NSE program and the one most often asked for in job postings. It validates the ability to install, configure, and operate a FortiGate Next-Generation Firewall running FortiOS in a day-to-day enterprise environment.

## What It Covers

- **Firewall policies and NAT** — building policy sets, source/destination NAT, and understanding policy lookup order.
- **Authentication** — local and remote users, LDAP/RADIUS integration, and captive portal.
- **VPN** — SSL VPN for remote access and IPsec site-to-site tunnels, including troubleshooting.
- **Routing** — static and dynamic routing, SD-WAN basics, and route lookup on FortiGate.
- **Security profiles** — antivirus, web filtering, DNS filtering, application control, IPS, and SSL inspection.
- **Logging and monitoring** — reading FortiGate logs, FortiView, and basic diagnostics from the CLI.

## Why It Matters

This is where the track meets my actual work: as an Implementation Engineer I deploy and secure network infrastructure for enterprise clients, and FortiGate is a common component of those designs. NSE 4 gives a structured, vendor-validated baseline for the configuration and troubleshooting I do in the field, and it is the prerequisite for the specialist NSE 6/7 tracks.
