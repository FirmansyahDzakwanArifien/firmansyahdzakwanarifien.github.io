## About the Certification

**Fortinet NSE 3 — Certified in Cybersecurity** is the level where the NSE program turns from general concepts to Fortinet's own architecture: the **Security Fabric** and the products that plug into it. It is about understanding the portfolio as a system — which product solves which problem, and how they share threat intelligence and policy.

## What It Covers

- **Security Fabric** — the integration layer that links FortiGate, FortiAnalyzer, FortiManager, and the wider portfolio into one visibility and automation plane.
- **Network security** — FortiGate NGFW, secure SD-WAN, and LAN edge (FortiSwitch, FortiAP).
- **Secure access** — SASE and Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA) for users who are no longer behind a single perimeter.
- **Endpoint and cloud** — FortiClient/FortiEDR and the cloud security offerings that protect IaaS, SaaS, and containers.
- **Operations** — how FortiAnalyzer, FortiSIEM, and FortiSOAR support monitoring and response at scale.

## Why It Matters

Much of the enterprise and service-provider market in Indonesia runs on Fortinet, so being able to speak the Security Fabric vocabulary fluently — in design discussions, in pre-sales, or when troubleshooting an integration — is a practical advantage for infrastructure and security engineering work. NSE 3 is the bridge between the conceptual levels and the hands-on FortiOS work in NSE 4.
